04

Reporting damage

Report damage to the common parts to the office as soon as possible.

  • Always report the damage to the office both by phone and by e-mail.
  • State the address of the building, where the damage is and the date you noticed it.
  • Add photos.
  • If the situation is urgent and the office is closed, call the on-call service.
